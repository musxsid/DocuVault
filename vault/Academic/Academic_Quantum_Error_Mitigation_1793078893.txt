QUANTUM ERROR MITIGATION IN HIGH-DIMENSIONAL HILBERT SPACES

Authors: Dr. Raymond Chen, Dr. Alistair Finch
DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevLett.128.090501
Date: 2026-09-18

Abstract: In this work, we demonstrate a scalable quantum error mitigation protocol designed for
noisy intermediate-scale quantum (NISQ) architectures. Using Clifford randomization and dynamic
decoupling sequences, we reduce circuit infidelities by 42% on superconducting transmon processors.

Keywords: Quantum Computing, Error Mitigation, Decoherence, Superconducting Qubits, Fidelity

References:
